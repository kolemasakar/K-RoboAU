## Signal
XAUUSD only. Evaluate each new M5 bar using closed chronological windows D1=60, H1=200, M5=300. EMA20 and EMA50 seeded by first period SMA; alpha=2/(n+1). BUY: close>EMA20>EMA50 and EMA20 exceeds value five bars earlier. SELL inverse.
H1 ATR14: average first 14 TR, then Wilder recursion. TR=max(H-L,abs(H-prevClose),abs(L-prevClose)). Latest closed H1 is continuation candidate. Search breakout newest first within last 12 bars, at least two bars before continuation. Level=max high or min low of preceding 20 bars. Breakout close strictly beyond level by 0.10 breakout ATR; directional body. Latest valid retest within next six bars, before continuation: BUY low within level +/-0.15 breakout ATR, close above level; SELL high within band, close below level. Continuation BUY close above retest high, own open and level; SELL inverse. Volume ratio vs preceding 20-bar median (1.5) and impulse body/range (60%) diagnostics only. No D1 ATR reachability gate in verified research path.
M5 confirmation latest closed candle starting at or after H1 continuation close. BUY close above previous five highs and own open, close location (C-L)/(H-L)>=0.70; SELL inverse and location<=0.30. Zero range invalid. Current replay enters at the confirmation cutoff; original frozen research entered five minutes later. MT4 uses the first available quote on the new M5 bar. Setup identity symbol/direction/breakout/retest/continuation; durable deduplication required.

## Exit and risk
BUY SL=retest low-0.10 continuation ATR; SELL SL=retest high+0.10 continuation ATR. Round structural SL away from entry. Reject distance >50 USD; never shorten structural SL to satisfy cap. TP=actual fill +/-3R. No trailing, partial close or position-age limit. Close EA-owned market positions before weekend; block entries during Friday cutoff and Saturday/Sunday. Provisional server-time Friday session close 23:45, lead 5 minutes (23:40 cutoff), configurable. Retry unsuccessful closes on subsequent ticks while blocked; no guarantee of execution without quotes or broker access. Holiday exceptions and actual broker schedule not validated.
GerchikCo DEMO initially; risk ceilings 5% per trade, 10% daily when today's closed-trade data are available, 10% aggregate. Daily budget subtracts realized gross losses and current open mark-to-stop risk. If daily data unavailable, log DAILY_RISK_UNAVAILABLE and apply only trade and aggregate ceilings. No full-history completeness audit. Round lots down, check margin and broker limits. Configure estimated round-trip cost per lot explicitly; default -1 blocks planning. Physical SL/TP required. AllowLiveTrading=false and EnableTrading=false by default. Source release lock remains false. Unknown mode fails closed. No credentials in public repo.
Historical research uses independent signal R outcomes and portfolio OHLC simulation; original frozen evaluator uses 168 wall-clock hours and partition censoring, 5 bps fee and 2 bps slippage per side. New portfolio simulation uses fixed 0.24 USD per lot, no slippage/swap/margin model, retrospective Friday last-bar proxy. Proxy is not a validated broker calendar. Historical results do not establish forward profitability.
